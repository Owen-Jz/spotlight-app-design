    Do I need experience to join a class?

    No. Every class has scaled options and coaches will walk you through each movement on your first visit.

    Is there a joining fee or contract?

    No joining fee. Monthly plans can be cancelled any time with 30 days' notice. Yearly plans are paid up front.

    How does the free trial work?

    Seven days of full Unlimited access. No card needed. We'll book you an intro session with a coach on day one.

    Can I freeze my membership?

    Yes, up to two months a year for travel or injury, free of charge.
